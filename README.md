One image that sums up the world news of Monday 23 November 2026. Under a
"THE WORLD TODAY — MONDAY 23 NOVEMBER 2026" banner, a hero panel shows the
COP31 climate talks in Türkiye ending without a deal, and nine labelled
scenes carry the rest of the day: a boy aged seven pulled alive from the
rubble of a collapsed Kyiv apartment block; a magnitude 6.5 earthquake in
the Vanuatu region; the Russian cargo ship Sparta II ablaze in the Baltic
with NATO helicopters dousing it; Democrats taking their largest US House
majority since 1992; Mexico's capture of cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro" in
Sinaloa; the UK confirmed as host of the 2035 Women's World Cup; the NHS
launching a major trial of AI to spot breast cancer earlier; an Israeli
leader condemning settler arson attacks in the West Bank; and Indonesian
rescuers recovering 24 victims of the Cikande candy factory fire. The
poster itself is `entry.png` in this repository; `POSTER-PLAN.md` records
how it was composed. The program is a standard-library-only Python copier:
it writes the shipped poster, byte for byte, to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
