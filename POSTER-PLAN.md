# Poster plan — the world on Monday 23 November 2026

One square front-page illustration. A deep-navy broadsheet banner across the top
carries the title "THE WORLD TODAY" and the dateline "MONDAY 23 NOVEMBER 2026"
in large cream letters, so the picture names its own day before anything else.

Below the banner, one wide hero panel, then a three-by-three grid of labelled
scenes. Every scene gets a short gold caption bar in bold capitals. The style is
a rich, flat editorial illustration: dense detail, clean shapes, no photographs,
no logos of real outlets, and every caption spelled exactly as written here.

## Hero panel
Caption: COP31 ENDS WITHOUT A CLIMATE DEAL
Scene: a vast summit hall in Türkiye at night. Rows of delegate desks face a
stage where two negotiators turn away from an unshaken handshake; behind them a
huge globe wrapped in a thermometer band pushing into the red, conference flags
hanging limp, papers scattered on empty chairs.

## Why these stories
They are the day's most consequential reports across regions and fields:
climate diplomacy, the Ukraine war, a Pacific disaster, a Baltic incident,
United States politics, Latin American crime, sport, health technology,
the Middle East, and a mass-casualty recovery in Indonesia.
